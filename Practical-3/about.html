About StudentHub

StudentHub is a responsive student portal designed using HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, CSS Grid and Flexbox. It provides one platform where students can register, participate in events, view academic information and stay updated with campus activities.

Our Mission

To simplify campus life through a modern and accessible web application.

Our Vision

To create an engaging digital environment that connects students with academic and extracurricular opportunities.
